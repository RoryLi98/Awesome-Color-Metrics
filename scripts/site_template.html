How this list was built

A GitHub survey on __CHECKED__ found no curated list dedicated to color metrics; the closest, Awesome-Image-Quality-Assessment, has a two-entry "Color IQA" subsection. Entries here come from the reference lists and citing papers of SPCD, CD-Flow, CD-iNet, MS-SWD, CD-Reasoning, ColorVideoVDP, HyAB, iCID and DeepSSIM, Crossref keyword searches, the evaluation sections of learned-ISP, burst and real-world super-resolution papers, ISO / IEEE camera standards, and common toolboxes.

Paper links were resolved through Crossref, arXiv or the publisher, and repository READMEs were read to confirm official code and package names, on __CHECKED__. Alignment labels follow what each paper claims or what its mechanism implies; “tolerates small shifts” means sub-pixel to a few pixels. A “partial” color label means the paper does not test color.

Known gaps: not a systematic review; IEEE Xplore and ACM DL were not searched exhaustively. Out of scope: no-reference quality models without a color component, color constancy algorithms, colorization and color transfer, and video-only temporal metrics. Found a gap or a wrong label? Contributions are welcome.
